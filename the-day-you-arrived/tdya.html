Earth Welcomes A New Light ~Samantha~
A tiny arrival creates a lifetime of memories
While the world continued with its usual headlines, millions of stories unfolding across the planet, one of the most important moments happened quietly.

On March 2nd 2007, while the world started doing its daily chores, it was unaware of the fact that it's greatest blessing just set her presence on Earth.

Entered the world with so much of charisma and beauty woven together, making hearts warm, making mouths smile like they've never did in their entire life.

Wrapped in a yellow cloth, her mum saw what the most beautiful human being in the world would look like, held by her nani and kissed, and the good news being shared to her father, immensely loved by her cousins.

The world kept moving that day. Songs played on the radio, movies filled theatres, and people went about their lives. But somewhere, a new laugh was heard for the first time. A new smile existed. A new chapter began. The biggest story of March 2nd, 2007 was never printed. Until now.

2nd March of 2007
Dearest Lil Samantha,

Thank you so much for being born. Right now, you probably don't know how much the world is going to change around you. You don't know the people you'll meet, the memories you'll create, or the lives you'll touch. You're just a little girl, starting the very first page of a story that will become so much bigger than you can imagine.

I hope you always keep the things that make you you. I hope you keep your laughter, even when life gets difficult. I hope you keep your kindness, even when the world gives you reasons not to.

I hope you never stop noticing the little beautiful things around you, because one day you'll realize those are the moments that make life truly special. And somewhere in the future, someone will look back at this little girl and be grateful that she existed.

Keep smiling. Keep being you.

With admiration and great affection,
A Well-Wisher
